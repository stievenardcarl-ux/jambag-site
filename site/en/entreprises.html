The JamBag show

A setlist built so the energy keeps rising, timeless hits in French and English, and an audience invited to sing from the first song to the last.

Quartet, or a tailor-made full band

The corporate jam

Get your colleagues involved.

The band opens and sets the mood, then invites your teams on stage. Your musicians pick up the bass or guitar, the shyest come up in groups of five to sing a chorus. Our musicians accompany everyone live, Carl hosts. No karaoke: real, spontaneous live music.

Beforehand: we find out who wants to play what, and the instruments are provided.

The live music quiz

The band plays, your teams guess.

A music game played live and hosted by Carl. Teams compete on the opening notes, then the whole room sings the chorus. Playful, unifying, perfect to break the ice.

Ideal for a team evening or a seminar

The acoustic break

An hour to sing together.

At lunchtime or at the end of the day, solo guitar and vocals or a cajon duo. A small sound system, mics passed around, and everyone sings, no fuss. Easy to organise, one-off or regular, and it brings teams closer.

Solo or duo, one hour, at your premises

Our producer

Live Tonight, behind the scenes

In France, a freelance performing musician must be employed by a production company. For JamBag, that's Live Tonight: even when you book us directly, invoicing and payment are handled with Clarisse.

Their commission, around 10%, is already included in our prices. For everything else, Carl remains your point of contact, from the first call to the big day.
