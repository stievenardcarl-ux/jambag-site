The ceremony

Emotion, above all.

Celebrant-led or religious: the entrance, the exchange of rings, the signing, the exit. You choose the songs that feel like you; we learn and arrange them for you. This is the acoustic moment, when the first tears appear.

Solo singer-guitarist · Acoustic duo

02

The drinks reception

Often the best moment of the wedding.

We build its highlights with you: the couple's arrival, the speeches, the group photo, the bouquet toss. The music first lets conversation flow, then gently builds.

And the last half hour has become our little tradition: everyone dances before sitting down to dinner.

Duo · Trio · Quartet

The evening party

A dance floor that never empties.

Two hours of live music to get three generations singing the same choruses, with your songs woven into the show. During dinner we leave room for conversation; when the live set ends, our partner DJs take over until the end of the night.

Questions fromCouples

Can you play our song for the entrance or the first dance?

Of course. Ceremonies are fully customisable, and for the party you can suggest up to five songs, or a special song we learn for you.

Do you play during dinner?

We play the ceremony, the drinks reception and the evening party. During dinner, a playlist or our partner DJ takes over.

Do we need to plan space and power?

A dedicated space for the band and a power supply nearby. Our technical rider covers everything, and we adapt to your venue.

What if a musician falls ill?

The concert goes ahead, whatever happens. Every musician has a dedicated stand-in within the JamBag family: we handle the replacement and always let you know. If a concert could not go ahead because of us, it would be fully refunded. It has never happened.

Do you travel abroad?

Yes: across France, Belgium, the rest of Europe and beyond. Travel costs are clearly stated in the quote.

Clarisse, JamBag's production manager at Live Tonight

Our producer

Live Tonight, behind the scenes

In France, a freelance performing musician must be employed by a production company. For JamBag, that's Live Tonight: even when you book us directly, invoicing and payment are handled with Clarisse.

Their commission, around 10%, is already included in our prices. For everything else, Carl remains your point of contact, from the first call to the big day.

  • Everything under contract
  • Client and band protected
