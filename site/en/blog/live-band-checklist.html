The quick checklist before your event with a live band

No time to read everything? Here are the essentials to tick off before your wedding or event.

The venue

  • Space for the band: about 6 × 4 m for a quartet, 3 × 2 m for a trio
  • Photos of the venue sent to the band
  • The band placed close to guests (and to the bar during a cocktail)
  • One power socket, ideally two, not shared with the caterer
  • The venue's sound limits (decibels, neighbours)

Sound and lights

  • Who provides sound and lights: the venue, our partner, or the band self-contained
  • The technical rider sent to the supplier

Timing

  • The band's arrival, three hours ahead on average
  • A slot for the soundcheck, with no guests or conference next door
  • Unloading and parking details

The schedule

  • The full programme shared with the band: speeches, photos, bouquet toss, games, awards
  • The songs that matter to you (entrance, first dance, songs to avoid)
  • The handover with the DJ, if there is one

Welcome

  • Musicians' meals (and dietary requirements)
  • A space to change, water during the show

For more detail, see our guide to hosting a live band.
