Good toKnow

Two hours of live music

That's our format: the right length to keep the intensity from the first song to the last. An hour and a half is possible, and the concert can run to three hours, with the extra hour charged separately.

Sound system included

Every line-up comes with its own sound system. The quartet comes with its sound engineer; for the other line-ups, we add one when the event calls for it. Sound and lights in detail

Travel

Included in the Lille area. Beyond that, travel costs, and accommodation if it's far, are set out in the quote.

Your songs

Suggest up to five songs and we'll work them into the show. For a song that needs a dedicated rehearsal, or beyond five, we look at it together.

Indicative prices for two hours of live music, sound system included, lights not included. Every event is unique: venue, timings, sound, travel. Your quote is tailored to it, and the more you tell us, the more it fits.

Our producer

Live Tonight, behind the scenes

In France, a freelance performing musician must be employed by a production company for every show. For JamBag, that role is played by Live Tonight.

Even when you book us directly, your date goes through them: payslips, declarations and the legal framework of the show. Their commission, around 10%, pays for that work. It is already included in our prices: the rates we quote include it.
