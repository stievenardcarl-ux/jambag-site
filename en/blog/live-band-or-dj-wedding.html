Live band or DJ for your wedding: how to choose

It comes up in almost every first call. The right answer is not "one or the other", but "who does what, and at which moment of the day".

What a band brings that no playlist can

A band does not just play songs: it reads the room. When guests are still hesitating to get up, we stretch a chorus everyone knows. When the dance floor is full, we keep going without letting the energy drop. A song can last two minutes or seven, depending on what is happening in front of us.

Watching musicians play also creates a gathering point. Grandparents come closer, children rush to the front, friends start filming. At almost every wedding we play, there is a moment when three generations sing the same chorus, and it rarely happens with a playlist.

And a band personalises: your entrance song, a track with a story for your family, a song learned just for you. That is when music becomes a memory, not just background.

What a DJ does better

Let's be honest: a DJ has the whole world's catalogue, can play all night without a break, and reproduces the exact sound of recent hits. Late at night, when the mood turns more club, a DJ is hard to beat.

Choosing your DJ (and your band)

"DJ" covers a lot. Between the host who runs games on the mic and the club DJ who mixes without a word, there is a world of difference. The right reflexes are the same as for choosing a band:

  • Read the reviews, not just the rating: what couples say about the night.
  • Look at photos and videos of real events, not just a playlist.
  • Judge the quality of your exchanges: do they reply quickly, clearly, do they listen? It is a good sign of what to expect on the day.
  • Check what is included: sound, lights, a mic for speeches.
  • Ask if they have worked with a band before: the handover needs preparing.

The combinations that work

A band makes the strongest impression when it plays at one precise moment: two intense hours, a lasting memory. The combinations our couples choose most often:

  • Ceremony and drinks reception with the band, then a DJ for the evening. This is where we play most often: the drinks reception is our favourite playground.
  • The start of the evening with the band, then a DJ until the end of the night.
  • The whole evening live with the full band: up to 7 musicians and several singers taking turns, for up to four hours. A rarer option, quoted on request, for those who want live music and nothing else.

When we work with a DJ, we prepare a smooth handover together: we plan the technical side so the band's space is cleared at the right time and the music never stops.

And you don't need a DJ for the after-party: the quartet can play up to three hours, then we leave Bluetooth speakers while we pack up, or we can rent you a sound system. Your guests carry on with their own playlist, and live music stays at the heart of the party.

Solo, duo, trio or quartet: no line-up is "better"

It is a matter of taste, not quality. A well-led solo guitar and vocals set can get 300 people singing and dancing: what matters is the ability to create an atmosphere, not the number of musicians. What changes from one line-up to another is the colour:

  • Solo: one voice, one guitar, a unique closeness. It fits anywhere, and many guests love this format for being different.
  • Duo: percussion drives the rhythm and fills the sound.
  • Trio: the bass arrives, and with it the groove. The sound gets richer and more danceable.
  • Quartet: drums, the band effect and the chemistry between musicians, for a very dynamic moment.

The more musicians, the fuller the sound and the more visual the "band" effect. But the best way to choose is to see and hear them: our videos show each line-up in action.

Three questions to help you decide

  1. What mood do you want? The softness of acoustic music, or something more energetic? With or without drums? It is a real question, and it changes the whole character of the moment.
  2. What place should live music have? The heart of your event, or a touch that accompanies it?
  3. Which moment do you want to make unforgettable? The ceremony, the drinks reception, the start of the evening: that is where live music belongs.

The simplest way is to talk about it: tell us about your day, and we will build the line-up that suits it.
