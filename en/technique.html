Good toKnow

Do we need to provide sound and lights?

All our line-ups come with their own sound system. If your venue or agency has a supplier, they set up from our technical rider: that's where the show is at its best. Otherwise we bring in our technical partner for stage, sound and lights, quoted separately.

Is a sound engineer included?

Yes with the quartet. For the solo, duo and trio, we add a sound engineer when the event calls for it: we make that call, to guarantee the quality of the concert.

How long does set-up take?

We usually arrive three hours ahead for set-up and soundcheck. One hour is sometimes enough when the sound is already in place.

What do you need on site?

At least one 230 V power point, ideally two, not shared with the caterer, and a performance area suited to the line-up. Everything is set out in the technical riders above.
